1Shuru Karne Se Pehle

2 minute ka kaam hai. Bas ye cheezein saamne rakh lijiye.

Aapko kya chahiye

  • Internet connection — WiFi ya mobile data, chalu hona chahiye.
  • Ek email ID — jo aap regularly kholte hain (OTP isi par aayega).
  • Ek mobile number — 10 digit ka, bina +91 ke.
  • Laptop/PC ya tablet — Chrome ya Edge browser me app best chalta hai. Mobile phone par bhi chalega, lekin bade screen par kaam aasan rehta hai.
  • Apne cafe ka menu — printed card, photo, ya Excel/CSV file — jo bhi ho.
  • Cafe ka logo (optional) — bill par chhapega.

App kaise kholein

  1. Apne laptop/mobile me Chrome ya Edge browser kholein.
  2. Address bar me PrabandhOS ka link type karein — ye link hamari team ne aapko WhatsApp ya email par bheja hoga.
    (Link na mila ho to 7689813155 par WhatsApp kar dijiye, turant bhej denge.)
  3. Enter dabaiye. Kuch second me PrabandhOS ka login page khul jaayega.
💡 Pro Tip — Link ko bookmark kar lijiye Page khulne ke baad keyboard par Ctrl + D dabaiye. Ab har baar link type nahi karna padega. Mobile par: browser menu → "Add to Home screen" — app jaisa icon ban jaayega.
📌 Yaad rakhein Pehli baar page thoda dheere khul sakta hai (5–20 second). Ye normal hai — server "jaag" raha hota hai. Doosri baar se fast chalega. Bar-bar refresh mat kijiye.

4Pehli Baar Ka Setup — Onboarding Ke 4 Steps

Account banne ke baad app khud aapko 4 chhote steps se guzarega. Har step ke neeche Continue button hota hai, aur upar Back — kabhi bhi peeche jaa sakte hain.

Step 1 — "Welcome Home." (Cafe ka naam & logo)

  • What's the name of your cafe? — cafe ka naam likhiye (jaise The Golden Bean).
  • Upload your logo — box par click karke apne logo ki image chun lijiye. Logo na ho to skip kar dijiye, baad me More → Cafe Settings se daal sakte hain.

Step 2 — "What's your style?" (Cafe ka type & serving)

  • Apne cafe ka type chuniye — Cafe, Restaurant, QSR, Bakery, jo bhi aap ho.
  • How do you serve customers? — yahan wo sab chuniye jo aap actually karte hain: Dine In, Takeaway, Delivery, Token/Counter.
    Ye important hai — jo yahan choose karenge, wahi options aapko POS screen par dikhenge. Baad me badalna ho to More → Cafe Settings → Order Types se ho jaayega.

Step 3 — "Build Your Menu"

Yahan 4 options milte hain. Poora detail agle Part 5 me hai — abhi bas itna samajh lijiye ki koi bhi chun lein, menu baad me kabhi bhi badla ja sakta hai.

Step 4 — "Build your crew" (Team add karna)

Aapka apna account upar ADMIN badge ke saath dikhega. Ab staff jodiye:

  1. Email or Phone — staff ka email ya mobile (jaise barista@cafe.com).
  2. Role — dropdown se chuniye: Manager, Cashier, Waiter, Chef, wagairah.
  3. App Login Password — unka password banaiye (kam se kam 6 characters). Ye password unhe bata dijiye.
  4. Screen Access — Automatic chun lijiye (role ke hisaab se screens apne aap set ho jaayengi). Custom tabhi chuniye jab aap khud ek-ek screen tick karna chahte hain.
  5. Add Team Member dabaiye. Aise hi baaki staff bhi jod lijiye.
💡 Jaldi me hain? Staff abhi skip kar dijiye — Continue dabaiye. Baad me kabhi bhi More → Team Portal → Staff Access se add kar sakte hain. Setup rukna nahi chahiye.

Chaaron step ke baad app aapko seedhe Menu screen par le aayega — taaki aap apna menu dekh/theek kar sakein.

Tarika 1 — Use a starter menu

Ek ready-made cafe menu turant chadh jaata hai — Espresso, Cappuccino, Latte, Cold Brew, Croissant, Muffin, Sandwich jaise 11 aam items, categories aur price ke saath.

  1. "Use a starter menu" card par click karein (border orange ho jaayega = select ho gaya).
  2. Continue dabaiye. Items add ho jaayenge.
  3. Baad me Menu screen par jaakar apne hisaab se naam/price badal lijiye, jo nahi chahiye wo delete kar dijiye.

Tarika 2 — Import from CSV (Excel se)

Agar aapka menu Excel ya Google Sheets me hai, to use CSV bana kar seedha upload kar dijiye.

Tarika 3 — Import from a photo

Apne printed menu ki photo daaliye, app khud items, category aur price padh kar bhar deta hai. Ye sabse tez tarika hai, lekin isme cross-check karna zaroori hai — poora detail agle Part 6 me hai. Wo part zaroor padhiye.

Tarika 4 — Start from scratch (khud add karna)

  1. Menu screen par upar-daayein + button dabaiye — Add Menu Item form khulega.
  2. Name — item ka naam (jaise Caramel Macchiato).
  3. Photo (optional) — "Tap to add a photo" par click karke item ki tasveer lagaiye.
  4. Category — Espresso, Food, Pastries… (nayi category bhi bana sakte hain).
  5. Price — sirf number (jaise 130).
  6. Subtitle / Description — optional, guest ko dikhta hai.
  7. Kitchen Station — ye item kis station par banega (Coffee Bar, Kitchen…). Isse hi KOT sahi station par jaata hai.
  8. Zaroorat ho to Add Variant (Half/Full, Small/Large) aur Add Option / Topping Group (Extra Cheese, Spice Level) bhi jod dijiye.
  9. Save dabaiye. Item menu me aa gaya.

6⚠ Photo Se Menu Upload — Zaroori Cross-Check

🔴 Sabse Important Baat — Ise Skip Mat Kijiye

Photo se menu upload karna bahut aasan hai, lekin ye 100% sahi nahi hota. Photo padhne wali technology (OCR/AI) kabhi-kabhi galti karti hai — naam adhoora padh leti hai, price ulta-seedha kar deti hai, ya do items ko mila deti hai.

Isliye upload ke baad har item ko apni aankh se check karna zaroori hai. Galat price ka matlab hai galat bill — aur galat bill ka matlab hai customer ke saamne sharmindagi aur paise ka nuksaan. 2 minute ka checking aapko baad ki badi headache se bacha lega.

Photo se upload kaise karein

  1. Onboarding me "Import from a photo" card par click karein.
    Ya baad me: More → Menu kholein aur upar wale banner "Import your menu from a photo" par click karein.
  2. Choose photo dabaiye.
  3. Apne menu card ki photo chun lijiye (ya mobile par camera se turant kheench lijiye).
  4. Thoda intezaar kijiye — ghoomta hua circle dikhega. App photo padh raha hai. Is beech page band mat kijiye.
  5. Neeche list aa jaayegi: 12 ITEMS READY · DOUBLE-CHECK THESE — ye "DOUBLE-CHECK THESE" likha hona hi ishara hai ki ab aapko check karna hai.

Achhi photo kaise lein (isse galtiyan aadhi ho jaati hain)

💡 Bade menu ke liye best tarika Ek saath poora menu daalne ki jagah, ek-ek category ki alag photo lijiye — pehle "Coffee" wala section, phir "Snacks", phir "Desserts". Har baar upload karke check kar lijiye. Isme accuracy bahut zyada hoti hai aur check karna bhi aasan.

Galti mile to turant theek kar lijiye (upload se pehle)

  1. Agar 3 se zyada items hain to "+ X more" par click karke poori list khol lijiye. Sirf pehle 3 dekh kar aage mat badhiye.
  2. Jis item me galti hai, us row par click kijiye — chhota Edit Item box khulega jisme Name aur Price hoga.
  3. Sahi naam/price type kijiye aur Save dabaiye.

Yahan poori list ache se dikhti hai. Ek final nazar zaroor daal lijiye.

Agar photo se kuch bhi nahi aaya

Aisa message aa sakta hai: "Couldn't find any menu items in that photo. Try a clearer, well-lit shot." Iska matlab hai app photo me se text padh hi nahi paaya. Ye kijiye:

  1. Achhi roshni me, seedha, saaf photo dobara kheenchiye (upar wali table dekhein).
  2. Poore menu ki jagah sirf ek section ki photo lijiye.
  3. Phir bhi na chale, to CSV wala tarika use kijiye — wo hamesha 100% sahi hota hai.
  4. Items kam hain to seedhe haath se add kar lijiye — 10 item me sirf 5 minute lagenge.
🔴 Golden Rule Photo se aaya hua menu kabhi bhi bina check kiye customer ko bill mat kijiye. Pehla order lene se pehle 2 minute nikaal kar poora menu ek baar dekh lijiye. Ye 2 minute aapko mahine bhar ki galtiyon se bacha lenge.

7Menu Item Edit Karna — Pencil Button

Galti baad me pakdi? Koi baat nahi. Menu kabhi bhi, kitni baar bhi badla ja sakta hai. Ye sabse zyada kaam aane wala chapter hai — photo upload ki galtiyan yahin se theek hoti hain.

Kisi bhi item ko edit kaise karein

  1. Neeche More tab par jaaiye.
  2. Operations section me Menu par click karein.
  3. Item dhoondhiye — upar Search menu items... box me naam type kar dijiye, turant mil jaayega.
  4. Us item ke card par pencil (✏) wala button dikhega — us par click kijiye.
  5. Edit form khul jaayega. Ab jo badalna ho badal dijiye:
    • Name — galat naam theek kijiye
    • Price — galat price theek kijiye (sabse zaroori)
    • Category — galat category me chala gaya ho to badal dijiye
    • Photo — item ki tasveer lagaiye/badliye
    • Subtitle / Description — guest ko dikhne wala text
    • Kitchen Station — KOT kis station par jaayega
    • Product Type / Veg-NonVeg — Veg, Non-Veg, Jain, Eggetarian
    • Variants — Half/Full, Small/Large aur unke alag price
    • Options / Topping Groups — Extra Cheese (+₹20), Spice Level, wagairah
  6. Save dabaiye. Badlav turant lag jaata hai — POS par bhi aur guest ke QR menu par bhi.
💡 Roz ka kaam — item khatam ho gaya to? Item delete mat kijiye! Use Unavailable mark kar dijiye. POS par wo UNAVAILABLE dikhega aur order nahi liya jaa sakega. Stock aane par wapas available kar dijiye — saara data (variants, recipe, price) bacha rahega.
📌 Ek saath bahut saare items theek karne hain? Agar photo upload me bahut zyada galtiyan aa gayi hain, to ek-ek karke theek karne se behtar hai: purane items hata dijiye aur CSV wala tarika use kijiye. Excel me ek baar sahi list bana lijiye — aage kabhi kaam aayegi.

9KOT — Kitchen Ko Order Bhejna

KOT hai kya?

KOT = Kitchen Order Ticket. Wo parchi jo kitchen ko batati hai ki kya banana hai, kitna banana hai, aur kis table/token ke liye. Bill customer ke liye hota hai — KOT kitchen ke liye. Dono alag cheezein hain.

KOT bhejna

  1. Cart taiyaar hone ke baad KOT & Print dabaiye.
  2. Dine In hai to table chuniye; Takeaway/Delivery hai to guest details bhar dijiye.
  3. Order kitchen chala jaayega aur printer se ticket nikal aayega (printer set na ho to bhi order KDS screen par pahunch jaata hai).
  4. Usi order me baad me aur item add karna ho? Table/Token dashboard se order kholiye, item add kijiye aur dobara KOT bhej dijiye — sirf naye items ka ticket jaayega.

KDS — Kitchen Ki Screen

Kitchen me tablet/screen ho to KDS tab kholiye. Parchi ki zaroorat hi nahi rahegi. Teen view milte hain:

ViewKya dikhata hai
KOT ViewHar order ki poori parchi — table/token ke saath. Normal kitchen ke liye best.
ItemsItem-wise list — "3 Cappuccino, 2 Latte" — ek jaisa kaam ek saath nipat jaata hai.
ProductionBulk banane ke liye — quantity ke hisaab se, purana order pehle (FIFO).

Upar All Stations se apni station chun lijiye (Coffee Bar, Kitchen, Tandoor…) — tab sirf usi station ka kaam dikhega. Kaam poora hone par item/KOT ko ready mark kar dijiye.

📌 Station kahan set hote hain Har menu item ka station uske edit form me hota hai (Kitchen Station). Naye station banane ke liye More → Cafe Settings → Stations. Printer settings ke liye More → Cafe Settings → Printers.

10Token Orders (Counter / QSR Style)

Jahan customer counter par order karta hai, token number leta hai, aur naam pukarne par le jaata hai — wahan ye screen use hoti hai.

Token order banana

  1. POS par order type me Token chuniye.
  2. Items add kijiye.
  3. Order fire kar dijiye. App turant token number de dega — jaise "Token #14 — sent to the kitchen."
  4. Token slip print ho jaayegi, customer ko de dijiye.
⚠ Token order rukta nahi hai Token orders turant kitchen chale jaate hain — inhe "hold" nahi kiya ja sakta. Agar customer abhi confirm nahi hai, to Save Draft use kijiye.

Token Dashboard

More → Token Orders — yahan aaj ke saare token orders ek saath dikhte hain.

  • Add Item — chalu token order me kuch aur jodna ho.
  • Print KOT — parchi dobara chhapni ho.
  • Cancel KOT — galti se gaya order kitchen se wapas lena ho.
  • NEW tag — jo items abhi tak kitchen nahi gaye.
  • + (FAB) — seedha naya token order shuru karne ke liye.

11Bill Banana & Settle Karna

Customer ne kha liya, ab paisa lena hai. Ise "Settle Bill" kehte hain.

Bill kaise settle karein

  1. Order kholiye — Tables se, Orders tab se, ya Token Orders se.
  2. Settle Bill dabaiye. Poora hisaab dikhega.
  3. Customer ko pehle bill dikhana ho to Print Bill dabaiye (ye "guest bill" hai — paisa lene se pehle wali copy).
  4. Payment Method chuniye: Cash, UPI, ya Card.
  5. Settle dabaiye. Bas — order complete, paisa record ho gaya.

Split payment — aadha cash, aadha UPI

  1. Payment Method me ek se zyada method select kar lijiye — jaise UPI aur Cash dono.
  2. Ek method me amount type kijiye (jaise UPI me 300).
  3. Bacha hua amount doosre method me apne aap aa jaayega.
  4. Cash zyada mila ho to neeche "Return ₹___" apne aap dikh jaayega — chhutta ginne ki zaroorat nahi.
  5. Sab match hone par Settle dabaiye.

Pay First (pehle paisa)

POS par Pay First dabane par wahi Settle Bill wala box seedha khul jaata hai — order banne se pehle hi. Isme ek extra tick-box hota hai: "Print KOT when settling". Ise tick rakhiye taaki paisa lete hi kitchen ko order chala jaaye. (Cash Sale me ye nahi aata — wo kitchen jaata hi nahi.)

💡 Bill WhatsApp par bhejiye Settle hone ke baad receipt par Send via WhatsApp button aata hai. Customer ka number daal kar bhej dijiye — paper bachega aur customer ka record CRM me bhi aa jaayega.

12Billing Page — Purane Bills Dekhna

Aaj kitna business hua, kis bill me kya tha — sab yahan.

Kaise kholein

More → Operations → Billing

Kya milega

  • TRANSACTIONS — upar summary: aaj kitne bill bane, kitna paisa aaya.
  • Recent Transactions — saare bills ki list, sabse naya sabse upar.
  • Kisi bhi bill par click kijiye — poori receipt khul jaayegi: items, Waiter ka naam, Subtotal, Bill Discount, Tax, Service/Packing/Delivery Charge, Tip, Round Off, TOTAL aur Paid Via (kis method se paisa aaya).
